The knowledge stack

Written once, checked twice, served grounded.

A corpus of 8,100+ pages compiles losslessly into an OWL 2 EL ontology — 286,533 machine-checked triples. At runtime, knowledge graphs populate it with live instances under Whelk's gate, and the Ontology Loom serves the checked graph into any model's context.

The published corpus is the ontology; the runtime graph is the knowledge graph.

Measured, not claimed

Grounding is evaluated. The numbers are published.

Serving a static ontology scaffold into the model's context lifts grounded recall to ~0.94 across every model tested (0.15→0.94 on Gemma, 0.27→0.94 on Muse, paired uplift with bootstrap 95% CIs), and it runs faster than the bare model. An input-exposure control shows the measured product is faithful delivery of curated, checked facts — a judged negative-control ladder confirms the lift is content-specific — the paper.
