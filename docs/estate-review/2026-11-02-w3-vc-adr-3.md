# VisionClaw ADR-2028 to ADR-2041

I traced all 209 headed sections of VisionClaw ADR-2028 to ADR-2041 against the source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. Eleven records are operative decisions; two of them (ADR-2037, ADR-2039) are still `proposed`. ADR-2040 (replaced by ADR-2112) and ADR-2041 (replaced by ADR-2115) are superseded. ADR-2031 is a withdrawn-number tombstone. Of the 195 sections below the titles, 50 are implemented as written, 27 are partly implemented, 3 are contradicted, 14 have been overtaken by a later section of the same record, and 15 are rationale or structure only. The other 86 are dated receipts about earlier commits. They cannot be checked at the pin, so each of their rows says only whether the current fact they assert still holds. The governed paths of ADR-2028, 2029, 2030, 2035, 2037, 2038 and 2041 are byte-identical between each record's `verified_commit` and the pin. The four XR records have drifted: ADR-2032, 2033, 2034 and 2036 declare no `verified_paths`, and their governed files have changed by about 4,000 lines since they were stamped. The most important findings are these. A wand-ray-driven `RadialMenu` builds its buttons in Godot's release mode, against ADR-2033. ADR-2030 describes only a build-time ISA rewrite, but the runtime loader applies a second one to every module it loads. ADR-2034 forecloses "any non-16 stride", but a correct stride-12 plane-edge buffer ships beside it. ADR-2038's Decision still promises an implicit `multi-user-locked` default, while the code refuses undeclared deployments.
