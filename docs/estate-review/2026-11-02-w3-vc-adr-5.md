# VisionClaw ADR-2074 to ADR-2106

I traced all 192 headed sections of the 31 VisionClaw records in group vc-adr-5 against the source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. For ADR-2084, 2085, 2102 and 2105 I also read agentbox at `c57d9c0508f4`. Classification: 29 are operative records, 26 accepted decisions and three still `proposed` (ADR-2102, 2103, 2104). ADR-2096 is superseded by ADR-2114, and ADR-2105 is historical (rejected). None is an imported copy. Each row below starts with one of eight dispositions, and the counts come from the tables. Title rows: 31. Implemented: 38. Partly: 16. Contradicted: 5 (current statements of fact that no longer hold, not reversed decisions). Overtaken by later code or a later record: 11. Rationale or context only: 38. Proposed and not implemented (for these I checked the gap claim instead): 8. Historical receipts that cannot be checked at the pin: 45. Where a receipt also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact holds. The most important finding is in ADR-2095, which is `complete`/`live` and says that no hand-formatted `urn:ngm:class:` mint remains in `src/` or `crates/`. At the pin, `crates/vault`, added after the record, hand-formats that IRI at two production sites, with 13 more in test modules across it and `crates/vault-core`. Neither crate depends on `visionclaw-domain`, so the record's own review trigger has fired. Second, ADR-2101's governance-case actor has changed without the record. A new `applying` claim state now prevents duplicate PRs. With no store, the actor refuses the PR, so it no longer fails open. A case interrupted while `applying` is never resumed and needs a manual fix. Third, ADR-2094 says `validate_security_env_vars` is the only reader of `MANAGEMENT_API_KEY`. `src/main.rs:731` reads it a second time with an empty-string default. Boot ordering currently masks that default.
