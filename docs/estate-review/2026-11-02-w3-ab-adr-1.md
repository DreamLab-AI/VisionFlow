# agentbox ADR-2001 to ADR-2013

I traced all 297 headed sections of agentbox ADR-2001 to ADR-2013 against the source at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. Eleven of the records are operative decisions. ADR-2002 (replaced by ADR-2153) and ADR-2005 (replaced by ADR-2036) are superseded. Of the 284 sections below the titles: 18 are implemented as written, 27 are partly implemented, 5 are contradicted, 6 were overtaken by a later section of the same record, and 14 are rationale or structure only. The other 214 cannot be checked at the pin, because they are dated receipts about earlier commit ranges. Where one of those receipts also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact still holds. The most important finding is that the two live ingress records, ADR-2009 and ADR-2010, still decide on the retired AoE daemon. They also overstate fail-closed behaviour in two places. If the boot projection of the config file fails, the NIP-98 door authenticates any validly signed key, although only the env-provided `/mgmt/` route survives a missing file. If the verifier cannot load, a session cookie still gets through. A third gap is in the relay consumer inside management-api: when its allowlist is empty, it admits every author. ADR-2011's recorded residual has been fixed in code, but the record still says otherwise.
