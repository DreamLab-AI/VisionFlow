# agentbox ADR-2062 to ADR-2092

I traced all 302 headed sections of the 28 agentbox records in group ab-adr-4 against source at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. The group has no ADR-2067, 2071 or 2081. Nineteen records are operative accepted decisions. Six are operative proposals: ADR-2073, 2075, 2076, 2077, 2085 and 2086. Two are superseded by ADR-2153 (ADR-2063 and 2079), and one is rejected (ADR-2074). None is an imported copy. By leading disposition, the 302 rows break down as follows:

- 72 implemented as written.
- 27 partly implemented.
- 6 contradicted at the pin: ADR-2063 Decision, ADR-2066 Decision, ADR-2070 Context and Decision, ADR-2078 Disposition, ADR-2090 Verification.
- 8 not implemented, matching their proposal status.
- 9 overtaken, superseded, rejected or resolved by other means.
- 136 cannot be checked at the pin, because they are dated runtime or diff receipts about earlier commits.
- 20 receipts whose stated current fact still holds.
- 17 accurate statements of absence, or test inventories that match source.
- 7 rationale-only or structurally defective sections.

The most important finding is in ADR-2062. It is `complete` and its gate is green, but only because it sanctions the three unauthenticated VNC servers. The shipped manifest's `x11-openbox` stack runs `Xvnc -SecurityTypes None`, which by source any container on `visionclaw_network` could reach. The authentication follow-on it hands to ADR-2040 is still open. The second most important is ADR-2070. Its named raw Loom egress door no longer has a first-party consumer: the `loom-raw` session now goes through the façade with the scaffold off, and the door's default URL points at a sidecar. The record, the egress register and the Context all still describe the old wiring.
