# visionGraph source admission and ontology header conversion — 1 November 2026

The space-corpus importer preserves useful review artefacts and performs several effective structural checks, but its successful exit is not evidence that the complete source-admission and semantic-conversion contracts have been met. This review adds [VG-07](../diagrams/visiongraph/07-reviewed-source-admission-and-header-import.md), five panels covering a previously unmapped path, and assesses every section of ADR-VG-005 and ADR-VG-006. It changes documentation only.

Source scope is visionGraph `556a0156b5c326fb61141ed2ab1760dd8d9c4dd6`, with the actual path dependency `project/crates/vault-core` exported at VisionClaw `75b72416bac176ca8fde715cac3bd7c77657b3e7`. Both are linked estate repositories. Sources were exported from commits, not from dirty working files. This pass covers the space/Earth intake; the later supply-chain source register and other ingestion programmes still need their own admission/evidence assessment. It does not authorise or execute a real `vault create`, `vault edit`, promotion, deployment or corpus rewrite.

## Comparison and utility for users

The pinned [SLSO source header](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nasa/LSDAO/830c437c013b52e881cbf42fe31f8727b92049cb/slso-base.owl) names its release and CC BY 3.0 terms; the pinned [SWEET licence](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ESIPFed/sweet/db60c8ddb1b781fbadae176f69286a2cdd5099a0/LICENSE) is CC0 1.0. These checks confirm the recorded labels for those exact upstream artefacts. They do not establish licence compatibility or completeness of attribution for every derivative.

[ROBOT extract](https://robot.obolibrary.org/extract.html) provides explicit module-extraction methods, import inclusion/exclusion and source annotations. Its locality modules aim to preserve entailments among selected entities. [ROBOT verify](https://robot.obolibrary.org/verify) runs SPARQL violation queries and fails by default when results are found. Those are useful comparison points for a declared semantic subset and executable acceptance rules, rather than a reason to assume that every RDF-to-Markdown transformation preserves OWL meaning.

My assessment is that visionGraph's useful distinction here is an editable Obsidian corpus with controlled local vocabulary, page-level lifecycle and publication integration. This importer is a project-specific reviewed-header tool; its inspected code does not establish a general lossless ontology-import product or semantic-module extractor. A curator should be able to answer three concrete questions before applying a batch: which source bytes were reviewed, which assertions were adopted or omitted, and exactly which pages will change. A digest-bound review receipt, a preview of semantic/visibility changes, and refusal of ambiguous or cyclic additions would make that workflow more useful and auditable. Measure success by preserved identities, explained omissions and successful user queries, not just generated page totals.
