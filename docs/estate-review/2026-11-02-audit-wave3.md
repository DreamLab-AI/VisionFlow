### Cross-topic consistency

The lead checked six confirmed findings against every topic that mentions the same mechanism. After the corrections above, no topic contradicts a confirmed finding. Remaining mentions either name the fact or describe a different thing: the dream-engine repository's crates are MIT OR Apache-2.0 at `59a3e215`, which is distinct from agentbox's own AGPL `services/dream-engine`.

### Cross-vendor spot-check

Codex (`gpt-6-astra`) reviewed 12 sampled panels, each with its cited source lines read at the declared revision. The first run was invalid because the sampler could not read agentbox through its `project/agentbox` submodule path or resolve the `visionclaw` revision key; both were fixed and the check rerun.

Result: **0 hold, 2 wrong, 10 cannot judge.** Both wrong findings were confirmed against the source and corrected (AB-13.4 and VC-34.9 above). The 10 cannot-judge verdicts are because a few lines either side of each citation are not enough to establish multi-step claims. This check is therefore **partial**: it found real errors at a rate of 2 in 12 in already-corrected panels, which is a caveat on the precision of individual panel details, and it did not independently confirm the other 10.

## Findings

The queue is `remediation/audit-wave3-2026-11-02.json`. The lead verified four against the source:

- **W3-01 (security, agentbox):** the management-api relay consumer admits every signed author when its allowlist is empty, and the shipped flake never sets that allowlist.
- **W3-02 (defect, VisionClaw):** saved API keys are written to one store and read from another that is never written, so they are never returned.
- **W3-03 (licensing, agentbox):** two AGPL crates were drawn as MIT/Apache; the panel is corrected.
- **W3-04 (drift, agentbox):** the licensing document claims a check that does not exist.

Seven more rest on the worker reports and are marked as such.

## What this wave does not claim

- No test, build, deployment or runtime check was run.
- The cross-vendor check is partial, as described.
- RuView stays excluded at the operator's direction.
- Ledger completion covers the ledger's denominators only. The other obligations in `completion/README.md` (whole-system path coverage, the SideStr and Hitch re-checks, cross-repository contract fixtures and the final synthesis) are still open.
