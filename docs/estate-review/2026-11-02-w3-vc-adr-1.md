# VisionClaw ADR-2001 and ADR-2003 to ADR-2013

I traced all 195 headed sections of the twelve VisionClaw records in group vc-adr-1 against the source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. ADR-2002 belongs to another group and is not assessed here. All twelve are operative decisions: none is superseded, and none is an imported copy. Each row below starts with one of seven dispositions, counted from the tables. Title rows: 12. Implemented: 38. Partly: 19. Contradicted: 4 (stale statements of fact, not reversed decisions). Overtaken by a later section of the same record: 12. Rationale or context only: 14. Historical receipts that cannot be checked at the pin: 96. Where a receipt also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact holds. The most important finding concerns ADR-2003, which is `complete`/`live` and says that deployments with private nodes "are protected without any configuration". That holds only on the WebSocket paths. `GET /api/graph/data` ignores the caller and runs a unit CQRS query that returns the repository's whole graph, and the export route's actor message carries no identity either, so every REST reader receives the whole graph, private nodes included. That means any signed-in Viewer, and in the dev compose profile, which defaults `RBAC_PUBLIC_READS=1`, any anonymous caller. The record's own 2026-09-05 section names this gap. Its status fields do not. Second, the operative Decision paragraphs of ADR-2009, 2010 and 2012 were never edited after later sections changed the facts, so the Decision text no longer states the implemented decision.
