# Audit wave 4: re-check of open SideStr and Hitch findings

None of the open findings in the six dated SideStr and Hitch reports of 1 November is closed in source. There are 25 rows, covering 26 finding IDs, because H1 and H3 are merged as they are in the source report. The reports were written at sidestr-rs `9c6de86c`. Between that revision and the wave-4 pin `814b9e54cd16`, the only change is one line in `docs/dream-cycle/LEDGER.md` (`git diff --stat 9c6de86c 814b9e54cd16`). For every finding, the line that shows the defect is still present at the pin, and each citation below was read there. Three findings also depend on consumer records. Those are still stale at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4` and VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. The evidence class is **source tracing at the pinned revision only**. No probe, test, build or runtime observation was rerun, so behavioural results such as "returns 43 instead of 42" are carried over from the original reports and not reproduced here. Two overlay items that their report had already resolved in the diagrams are listed separately. The source still matches the corrected wording.
