# VisionClaw ADR-2042 to ADR-2072

I traced all 193 headed sections of the 27 VisionClaw records in group vc-adr-4 against source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. The group has no ADR-2050, 2051, 2052 or 2062. Twenty-six records are operative accepted decisions: 22 marked `live` and four `staged` (ADR-2043, 2049, 2061 and 2071). One is superseded: ADR-2042, by ADR-2113. None is an imported copy or a support file. By leading disposition, the 193 rows break down as follows (titles are counted under the disposition of their decision):

- 69 implemented as written at the pin, including two whose outcome is runtime-only and one with a stale sentence.
- 13 partly implemented, or amended or drifted in mechanism.
- 6 contradicted at the pin: ADR-2048 Decision and Consequences, ADR-2060 Decision, ADR-2070 Decision and Consequences, and ADR-2043 Consequences (its last bullet).
- 6 overtaken by later decisions, or superseded, in whole or in part.
- 40 dated receipts whose stated current fact still holds at the pin.
- 30 historical receipts (dated test, build or runtime runs on earlier trees) that cannot be checked from source.
- 28 context sections whose premise describes pre-change code now absent, consistent with the pin.
- 1 rationale-only section.

The most important finding is that three of the four documentation-correction records in this group no longer hold. ADR-2060 says it repointed the V5 envelope citation in `BASELINE-architecture.md` at the graph decoder and gave the envelope an owner. At the pin, BASELINE still cites the settings-socket file `binary_settings_protocol.rs` and still says "the V5 envelope has no owning ADR". ADR-2070 says the wire-id release-truncation hazard was corrected in `IDENTIFIER-taxonomy.md`. That document marks the divergence resolved at line 267, but its own Invariant 6 at lines 238–242 still says release builds silently truncate. ADR-2048's corrections have drifted back onto unrelated lines: BASELINE's `RBAC_PUBLIC_READS` citation `docker-compose.unified.yml:93` now lands on `VITE_HMR_PORT`. The second most important finding is ADR-2049. Its fetch gate is real, but the stub-stage build line pipes through `tail` with no `pipefail`, so its explanatory fallback can never print. The Dockerfile comment's claim that "the DEPENDENCY build must succeed" is not enforced by any command.
