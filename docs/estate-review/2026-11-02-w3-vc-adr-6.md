# VisionClaw ADR-2107 to ADR-2133 (group vc-adr-6)

I traced all 206 headed sections of the 19 VisionClaw ADRs in group vc-adr-6 (ADR-2107 to ADR-2119, ADR-2124 to ADR-2128 and ADR-2133) against the source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. Corpus claims were read at visionGraph `556a0156b5c3`, and two cross-repository pins were checked at nostr-rust-forum `c39f41f104b1` and loom `ba21a689a57d`. All 19 are operative decisions. ADR-2108 and ADR-2111 are still `proposed`, and ADR-2111 is unbuilt by its own account. None is superseded or imported. For every record with an armed `verified_paths`, the governed files are byte-identical between its `verified_commit` and the pin, so what follows is drift in the records' text, not churn in their code. Of the 206 sections: 49 are implemented as written, 35 partly, and 1 is contradicted outright (ADR-2108's "known defect, not fixed", which ADR-2119 fixed). 3 are not implemented, as the proposed ADR-2111 says itself. 6 are overtaken by a later section of the same record, and 4 are accurate status or scope statements. 36 are title, rationale or continuation headings. The other 72 cannot be checked at the pin: they are dated receipts, test counts or runtime observations. Where one of those also states a current fact, its row says whether the fact holds. The most important finding is ADR-2112, the corpus-format decision. Three of its commitments are contradicted inside a row marked "partly". `vault validate` has no rule for three of the eight Logseq constructs D5 says it rejects, and no code emits the `RESOURCE_MUTATED` blocker behind D7's immutability check. D11's "`knowledge/assets` is a symlink into `working/assets`" is inverted in visionGraph: `knowledge/assets` is a real 148-file directory beside a 567-file root store, which is the two-tree arrangement D11 was written to prevent.
