# agentbox ADR-2140 to ADR-2164 and imported example ADRs

I traced all 270 headed sections of the 23 records in group ab-adr-7 against agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. The most important finding is that two records disagree with the code on status and intent. ADR-2151 still says voicelink is `proposed` with no implementation, but the pinned manifest enables it and the flake bakes it. ADR-2155's Decision still promises that consultant, trajectory, ontology, governance and dream-triage content stays on the local route; since the owner's 10-30 amendment, `local_only = []` with a cloud default backend sends all of them to the selected vendor. There are 16 agentbox records with 148 sections: 15 operative decisions, plus ADR-2152, which ADR-2154 supersedes. Of those sections, 33 are implemented, 18 partly implemented, 4 accurate, 6 overtaken by a later pin or amendment, 3 superseded, 2 contradicted and 2 rationale only. The other 80 cannot be checked at the pin, because they are dated receipts or runtime evidence. The seven imported copies have 122 sections: the BHIL example and ruvector-catalog ADR-001 to ADR-006. They are not agentbox decisions and agentbox adopts none of their obligations, so their 116 upstream sections are marked imported, not adopted. Agentbox wrote the six "Closeout extension" sections in them, and I assessed those against source: five are accurate and one is partly stale. Other findings: a bulk re-stamp carried ADR-2141 and ADR-2143 across the unrecorded dream-core 0.4.0 adoption; the video-production skill ships `live` before the route-eval its record requires; and the retained Blender bridge, which executes Python, is published on `0.0.0.0:9876`.
