# agentbox ADR-2030 to ADR-2061 (group ab-adr-3)

I traced all 264 headed sections of the 26 agentbox records in group ab-adr-3 against the source at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. For ADR-2060 and ADR-2061 I also traced VisionClaw at `86cdd7635582`. The 100 unverifiable sections are mostly dated receipts about earlier commits; where such a receipt also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact holds.

The most important finding concerns ADR-2040's rule that no supervised program offers an unauthenticated listener on a bridge-reachable interface. Four programs at the pin break it, and the CI gate sanctions all four rather than failing:

- the three desktop VNC servers, which the record already lists as open;
- `system-one-proxy`, which is not in the record.

`system-one-proxy` is enabled in the shipped manifest with `network = true`. It binds `0.0.0.0:8094` on `visionclaw_network` and authenticates no caller. It forwards every request with the backend vendor key. Any container on that network can therefore spend the operator's model credentials. That listener landed on 2026-10-30, and the record's review trigger fired without a re-review.
