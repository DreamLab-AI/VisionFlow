# agentbox ADR-2093 to ADR-2118 (group 5)

I traced all 298 headed sections of the 23 agentbox records in group 5 (ADR-2093 to ADR-2118) against source at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. Where a record makes claims about other repositories, I also read solid-pod-rs `9b6143ce55b2`, VisionClaw `86cdd7635582` and nostr-rust-forum `c39f41f104b1`. Twenty-two records are operative decisions: thirteen accepted and nine proposed (the sidestr settlement pack 2096 and 2098-2103, plus 2104 and 2117). One record, ADR-2110, is historical, rejected after its EXP-B8 verdict. The group contains no imported copies and no support files. Of the 298 rows, 23 are titles. Of the other 275: 74 are implemented as written, 35 are partly implemented, 25 are overtaken by a later section or later code, and 31 are rationale or structure only. Sixteen are not implemented, which is consistent with their proposed or rejected status, and one is contradicted outright. The other 93 cannot be checked at the pin, mostly dated receipts about earlier commits; where one of those receipts also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact holds. Six of the "partly" rows contain a contradicted clause: ADR-2096 D6, ADR-2098 D3, ADR-2100 D1, ADR-2101 D3, ADR-2102's research-stage bullet and the ADR-2107 Decision. The one contradicted outright is ADR-2094's Verification. The most important finding is E1. ADR-2100 requires every chain spend to pass the `payment_settlement` authority gate "from the first commit of `/v1/chain/*`", but the live `POST /v1/chain/pay` rail settles sidechain transactions through spend-policy and a NIP-98 approver park. It never calls `lib/authority.js`. An owner decision (Q14) excuses this for valueless test coins. The code ties the rail only to testnet parents (`SIDESTR_CHAINS`, E-PAY5), and ADR-2103's own amendment says a testnet parent is "never the proxy for no value". The second is E6: a registered estate MCP server (`substrate-tools`) still reads the sovereign corpus through a hand-rolled parser, which goes against ADR-2107's "Forbidden" clause and ADR-2108's premise that the parser has no agent-facing caller.
