# agentbox ADR-2014 to ADR-2029

I traced all 304 headed sections of agentbox ADR-2014 to ADR-2029 against the source at agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. For ADR-2025 I also read VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. Fifteen of the records are operative decisions; three of those (ADR-2025, 2026 and 2027) are still `proposed`. ADR-2022 is superseded by ADR-2108. None is an imported copy. Of the 304 rows:

- 42 are implemented, or accurate as stated.
- 41 are partly implemented. These include the four contradictions this review found.
- 21 were overtaken by a later section of the same record.
- 22 are a title, rationale or structure only.
- 6 are historical rows of the superseded ADR-2022.
- 1 describes a lifecycle that has not been built.
- 171 cannot be checked at pin, because they are dated receipts about earlier commits. Where one of those receipts also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact still holds.

The most important finding is that ADR-2014's core rule, that durable memory is written only through the governed path, is broken by a live writer. The management-api memory adapter `ExternalPgMemoryAdapter` is the configured slot. It upserts into `memory_entries` with no embedding, which produces exactly the HNSW-invisible rows the record exists to prevent. It is reachable through the `/v1/memory` route, which `management-api/server.js:399` registers.

The second most important is that ADR-2020's "never auto-routed" does not hold. The runtime skill-routing hook offers `tree-search-coder` as a candidate, because the only thing stopping it is the skill's own prose. Its spend cap is enforced, though.
