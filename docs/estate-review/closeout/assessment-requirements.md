## Evidence needed to finish the review

For each material contract, either provide the applicable source/execution/deployment evidence or state precisely which evidence is missing and why that limits the assessment. A complete-system implementation claim requires the complete journey; a source-level gap claim may be established with a bounded source trace or isolated counterexample. Do not rerun production actions merely to make a documentation status green.

Every remaining ADR needs an explicit outcome: operative and evidence-qualified; proposed and awaiting adoption; superseded with traceable replacement; historical with continuing obligations; imported with consumer relevance and ownership limits; or support/content rather than a decision. Preserve existing dates and archival restrictions. Unknown is a valid interim state but not completion of the all-record review.

The active goal should remain open while these assessment tasks remain. The roadmap is allowed to contain open implementation work; the review must still be complete and candid about that work before claiming its own completion.
