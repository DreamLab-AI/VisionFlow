# VisionClaw boot, actor supervision and request lifecycle (wave 3)

I assessed all 41 panels of three VisionClaw topics by source tracing at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`: `visionclaw/01-boot-and-app-state` (13 panels), `visionclaw/02-actor-supervision` (14) and `visionclaw/03-request-lifecycle-and-rbac` (14). All three topics were stamped at `8692b12ba987`, which is 217 commits behind the pin, so I restamped each one to `86cdd76355823a51bb64d503c005fe277726e724` and re-resolved every citation. Of the 41 panels, 11 are accurate, 25 corrected, 5 accurate with limits and none inaccurate-unfixed. The most important finding is in topic 02 and reaches into topic 01. Under the ADR-2142 amendment of 2026-11-01, swarm rosters, the `/bots/data` view and the S5 payment projection moved from `GraphStateActor` to `AgentBeamActor`, and `AppState` now keeps the `AgentBeamActor` address and registers it with the supervisor. The topics still drew the old routing and an actor "with no Addr held".

## Findings

**F1. Bots-roster routing moved to AgentBeamActor; topics 01 and 02 drew the old path (corrected).**
- *Trigger:* VisionClaw commits between `8692b12` and `86cdd76` (ADR-2142 amendment, 2026-11-01).
- *Consequence:* VC-02.3 routed `GetBotsGraphData`, `UpdateBotsGraph` and `UpdateChainPayments` to `GraphStateActor`. VC-02.1 and VC-02.20 said `AgentBeamActor` keeps no address and lives only through its hub subscription. VC-01.5 omitted the new `SetAgentBeamAddr` wiring. A reader following those panels would look for the bots graph in an actor that no longer holds one.
- *Evidence at 86cdd76:*

  - `UpdateBotsGraph` now carries `source` and `targets` (`src/actors/agent_monitor_actor.rs:714-718`).
- *Closed by:* edits to VC-01.5, VC-02.1, VC-02.2, VC-02.3 and VC-02.20, plus a **Drift** marker at the end of topic 02.
- *Beyond my assignment (not edited):* the same identifiers appear in `docs/diagrams/visionclaw/04-handlers-graph-and-state.md`, `07-hexagonal-ports-and-crates.md`, `27-agent-integration-mcp-relay.md` and `34-client-features.md`. Their owners should check them against this routing change.
