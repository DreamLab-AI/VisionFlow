# Loom and dream-machine ADRs

All 16 numbered decision records in loom (ADR-135 to ADR-142) and dream-machine (ADR-0001 to ADR-0007 and ADR-DL-001) were assessed section by section against source at the pinned revisions. Of 234 headed sections: 90 implement what they say; 97 are partly true; 7 are contradicted; 6 describe machinery that does not exist; and 34 cannot be checked from source. The most important finding is ADR-135 D7, which sets the trust boundary for distilled knowledge. It requires a BIP-340 envelope signature and a distiller allowlist, and says generic `power_user` must not suffice to write `:summary`. VisionClaw's derived-write door is gated by `power_user` alone and checks no signature (LOOM-A-01). Two further mismatches matter.
- VisionClaw still performs the CLEAR+INSERT that ADR-135 D2.3 retires, and its own ADR-2017 re-affirms it (LOOM-A-02, a cross-repository **Tension**).
- ADR-141's promised refusal of a marker whose content digest disagrees is dropped by the Rust reload port (LOOM-B-01).

On dream-machine, the darwin path enforces an unrecorded "ADR-0008" (DM-02). ADR-0005 cites an agentbox admission file that no longer exists (DM-01). The rest of the dream-machine series is largely implemented, through a chain of amendments.

Disposition vocabulary, used as the first word of every cell:
- **Implements:** the section's mechanism claims hold in source. For an Alternatives section, it means the rejected option is absent.
- **Partly:** some claims hold and others do not; the cell names which.
- **Contradicts:** source shows the opposite of the section's claim. A section whose text is stale but whose other claims hold is **Partly**.
- **Not implemented:** the described machinery does not exist, and the record is Proposed or phased.
- **Cannot be checked:** a history, external, runtime or deployment claim.

## Findings

### Loom ADR-135 to ADR-139

- **LOOM-A-01: the `:summary` write door accepts any `power_user`; ADR-135 D7 forbids exactly that.** Trigger: a caller holding generic `power_user` posts summary quads. Consequence: the distillate trust boundary ADR-135 D7 calls binding (BIP-340 envelope signature checked against a distiller allowlist, and "generic `power_user` must NOT suffice to write `:summary`") does not exist. The graph fence does exist: `FORBIDDEN_GRAPHS` / `ALLOWED_GRAPHS` block `:assert`/`:inferred`. Evidence: VisionClaw 86cdd76 `src/handlers/ontology_derived_handler.rs:30`, `:36`, `:188` (`.wrap(RequireAuth::power_user())`); no signature, Schnorr or allowlist reference in that handler, and no BIP-340/Schnorr/allowlist/envelope verifier on any derived-write path in VisionClaw `src` or `crates` (the only BIP-340 hit is an unrelated canary, `src/services/canary_nostr_tap.rs:218`). Note that `ALLOWED_GRAPHS` is `:summary` + `:observed`, not the `:summary`/`:usage` pair the ADR names. Close: either implement envelope verification against a distiller allowlist at the derived door, or amend ADR-135 D7 to the posture actually shipped.
- **LOOM-A-02: CLEAR+INSERT is live in VisionClaw, and VisionClaw's own ADR re-affirms it.** Trigger: any `force_full_sync`. Consequence: ADR-135 D2.3 ("RETIRED") is contradicted, and the contradiction is an open cross-repository decision conflict rather than unbuilt work: VisionClaw ADR-2017 states the `:assert` projection "is regenerated by full `CLEAR + INSERT DATA`" and that this "holds exactly as before". Evidence: VisionClaw 86cdd76 `src/services/github_sync_service.rs:406-407`; `docs/adr/ADR-2017-write-master-backup-posture.md:216`; no `/loom/generation` or Loom client anywhere in VisionClaw `src`. **Tension:** ADR-135 D2.3 vs VisionClaw ADR-2017. Close: supersede ADR-135 D2.3 explicitly (ADR-141 / VisionFlow ADR-2013 moved authority to `vault build`), or reopen ADR-2017.
- **LOOM-A-03: ADR-135's façade contract and distillation pattern have no implementation.** No `/loom/v1/*` route exists; the served routes are `/health`, `/loom/generation`, `/loom/scaffold`, `/loom/sparql`, `/loom/search`, `/loom/search/semantic`, `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/models`, `/loom/attest`, `/loom/attest/verify` (loom ba21a68 `crates/loom-facade/src/routes/mod.rs:54-74`). No distil submit/status/result route, no `jobd`, and no `job` URN kind: agentbox c57d9c0 `management-api/lib/uris.js:88` `KINDS` has no `job` entry; `ontology_distill_*` and `lease_epoch` appear only in agentbox `docs/`. `best-answers` (`crates/loom-facade/src/bin/best_answers.rs:1-22`) is a bench-row collector for a later PRD-025 amendment, not the D4 job pattern. Close: retire D1.1/D4/D5 or move them to PRD-025, as the 2026-10-02 disposition already proposes.
- **LOOM-A-04: ADR-137 D1's "one static musl binary" is not what ships.** The image builds on `rust:1.97-bookworm` and runs on `debian:bookworm-slim`; the Dockerfile itself records why musl was abandoned (RocksDB/C++ toolchain). Evidence: loom ba21a68 `deploy/Dockerfile:16-29`, `:39`, `:118`. **Drift:** ADR-137 D1, §4 Positive and §8 D1 verification still state musl. Close: amend the ADR text.
- **LOOM-A-05: ADR-137 D6's `sqlx` choice was not taken; the rejected alternative was.** No `sqlx` in any manifest; the off-turn Postgres channel is `tokio_postgres` and is read-only (export from PG into a local `.rvdb`, "never writes to PG"). Evidence: loom ba21a68 `crates/loom-vector-ruvector/src/bin/export_corpus.rs:1-13`, `:129`; `Cargo.toml:40-66` (no sqlx). **Drift:** the ADR describes a `sqlx` embed-on-promote write channel. Close: amend D6.
- **LOOM-A-06: ADR-137 D2's "clamps as tower layers" is not how the code is built.** The tower layers are timeout, body limit, CORS and trace (`routes/mod.rs:77-83`); the SPARQL clamp is a plain function in the adapter (`crates/loom-graph-oxigraph/src/lib.rs:104`). Mechanism differs, the guarantee holds. **Drift.**
- **LOOM-A-07: the domain predicates are not in `loom-domain`.** ADR-136 D5 and ADR-137 D9 say subclass-acyclicity, duplicate-label, type-match and relation-contradiction "stay Loom-owned in `loom-domain`". None exists there (grep for the predicate names and for `predicate` in `crates/loom-domain/src` finds only the relation-kind vocabulary at `model.rs:82-149`). The Python `app/pipeline/*` that held them was removed (only `app/mirror.sh` remains under `app/`). The ledger exists (`crates/loom-attest-proofgate/src/lib.rs:172` `ChainedLedger`, SHA-256 at `:65`) but has no in-repo predicate producer. Close: name where the predicates live now (presumably the `vault build` / ontomark gate under ADR-141) and amend D5/D9.
- **LOOM-A-08: stale counts and labels in ADR text (Drift).** "Eight-crate workspace" (ADR-135 implementation note, ADR-137 status, §4, Closeout) against eleven members (`Cargo.toml:3-15`, adding `loom-client`, `loom-mcp`, `loom-bench`). "Six answer paths" (ADR-136 and ADR-138 acceptance progress) against eight `GroundingStatus` variants (`crates/loom-domain/src/grounding.rs:310`, now including `passthrough` and `navigated`). `corpusNature` is named as part of the canonical unit in THE PRIZE and Invariant I-P1 of ADR-136/137, but `CanonicalUnit` now carries `provenance: Provenance` instead, by ADR-140 D4 as amended (`crates/loom-domain/src/model.rs:59`, `:72-76`). ADR-136 §1's "no `.github/workflows/`" is overtaken by `.github/workflows/rust-contracts.yml`.
- **LOOM-A-09: `app/mirror.sh` still promotes file at a time.** It is the remaining-work item ADR-135's 2026-09-05 progress records, still open: `os.replace` per file, then the marker last (`app/mirror.sh:150` onward). The Rust `BundlePromoter` (`crates/loom-facade/src/bundle.rs:272`) is not used by the shell mirror. Close: port the mirror onto `BundlePromoter`, or retire the shell script.

### Loom ADR-140 to ADR-142

1. **LOOM-B-01: ADR-141 promises a content-digest refusal that the Rust reload port deliberately drops.**
   Trigger: an `ontomark build` marker whose declared `content_digest` disagrees with its artefact set, while every listed artefact hashes correctly. Consequence: ADR-141 Decision 6 says the reloader refuses this. The port that replaced the Python script carries the declared digest and then discards it without comparing (`crates/loom-facade/src/reload.rs:207`). It also returns its own recomputed digest (`reload.rs:248`). A bundle whose marker misdescribes its source corpus therefore activates. The per-file hashes still catch a tampered artefact list. Evidence: `docs/design/ADR-141-loom-consumes-the-vault-build.md:79-81` against `crates/loom-facade/src/reload.rs:207-210`, `:248`; the declared digest is parsed at `crates/loom-facade/src/mirror.rs:436`. ADR-141 Verification item 3 cites `tests/test_reload_published_generation.py`, which is absent from the tree, as is `scripts/reload-published-generation.py`. `crates/loom-facade/src/reload.rs:8` names the latter as ported. To close it, either restore the comparison with a test, or amend ADR-141 D6 and Verification 3 to the Rust behaviour and its tests.

2. **LOOM-B-02: ADR-140's realisation and verification sections describe P2–P4 machinery as if specified for the tree, but none of it exists.**
   Trigger: a reader takes ADR-140 §3.2/§3.3/§3.4/§7 as the current design. Consequence: `ProbeExecutor`, `TrajectoryStore`, `ExposureProfileStore`, `ExposureProfile`, `loom.probe`, the `loom-evolve` crate, `exposure-profiles/` and `PatchProposal` ledger entries do not exist. A `git grep` finds no definition at the pinned revision. The only `PatchProposal` hit is a doc comment, `crates/loom-facade/src/routes/attest.rs:80`. The ports file has only `ManifestSource` among the new ports (`crates/loom-domain/src/ports.rs:137`). `/mcp` has six tools, not seven (`crates/loom-mcp/src/schema.rs:51-127`). `/health` does not report plane readiness, exposure profile or coverage, which is Verification item 3 (`crates/loom-facade/src/routes/health.rs:30-70`). The ADR's own notes say P2 onward is not started (`ADR-140…md:634`), so this is honest phasing, not a false claim. Some body text was not amended, though, and still reads as delivered contract. To close it, mark §3.1–3.4 and §7 rows by phase (P0 shipped / P2+ unbuilt).

3. **LOOM-B-03: D4's Mapping and AttestedComputation reach the index and coverage counts but not the served unit's header.**
   Trigger: ADR-140 D4 says "Its typed header gains two families". Consequence: `CanonicalUnit` has `provenance` but no mapping or attested-computation fields (`crates/loom-domain/src/model.rs:59-78`). The families are carried only by the producer-typed `ClassEntry` (`crates/loom-scaffold/src/index.rs:16-17`) and counted in the manifest coverage (`crates/loom-scaffold/src/manifest.rs:151-161`). An agent calling `loom.resolve` cannot see a Mapping, and `UnitKind::Mapping` browse falls back to kind-agnostic `seeds` (`crates/loom-domain/src/ports.rs:38-46`). To close it, record in D4 that header projection is P2 work, or add the fields.

4. **LOOM-B-04: ADR-142 is internally inconsistent about what is implemented and active.**
   Trigger: front matter `implementation_status: partial`, `activation_status: inactive`, against the body. Consequence: the Decision (line 27) and the first Verification paragraph (line 44) say step-10 is implemented. Line 49, a surviving pre-step-10 paragraph, says "No implementation or activation is claimed." The 2026-10-12 section records a first served `ontomark build` bundle via the timer (lines 56-58). The ADR's own activation rule (line 30) makes that the trigger for `active`, yet the front matter stays `inactive` with `verified_commit` empty. Two of the three named migration tests (`ontomark_iri_migration`, `ontomark_rdf_migration`) do not exist; only `crates/loom-domain/tests/ontomark_cli_publish_migration.rs` does. This is consistent with "IRI and provenance adoption remain separate migrations". To close it, delete line 49 and either flip `activation_status` or state why the 10-12 served receipt does not count.

5. **LOOM-B-05: ADR-142 Decision items 3 and 4 have no source support.**
   Trigger: "Consumers negotiate or reject unsupported contract versions explicitly" and "Report the loaded generation and ontology version IRI". Consequence: the strict marker parser requires `vocabulary_version` to be present but never range-checks it (`crates/loom-facade/src/mirror.rs:402-417`). No `versionIRI`/`version_iri` is read or reported anywhere under `crates/`. A future vocabulary bump would be accepted silently. To close it, add a supported-version check and an ontology-version-IRI field to the loaded identity, with the rejection tests the ADR itself lists (line 44).

6. **LOOM-B-06: ADR-141's `wasDerivedFrom` rule is a producer and document contract only, with no Loom code that emits or checks it.**
   Trigger: ADR-141 Decision 1, "`wasDerivedFrom` names the local repository … never a GitHub URL". Consequence: no `wasDerivedFrom` occurrence exists under `crates/`. Only the pipeline doc states the rule (`docs/design/ONTOLOGY-LOOM-PIPELINE.md:22-23`, `:111`). Loom neither enforces nor reports it, and the 2026-10-12 amendment (lines 152-160) has since separated identity from distribution. Classed as cannot-be-checked from Loom source. To close it, name the producer as the owner in the ADR, or add a marker check.

7. **LOOM-B-07: minor drift.**
   (a) ADR-140 §"THE PRIZE" says it is "quoted verbatim" from `ddd-ontology-loom-context.md`. The source text differs: a paraphrase with different wording (`docs/design/ddd-ontology-loom-context.md:13`). (b) D1 says both planes stamp "the same six statuses (ADR-138)". `GroundingStatus` now has eight variants, including `OptOut` and the ADR-140-added `Navigated` (`crates/loom-domain/src/grounding.rs:310-340`). (c) §3.1 `Manifest.content_digest` is not a manifest field. The digest rides inside `Generation.content_digest` (`crates/loom-domain/src/manifest.rs:126-137`; `crates/loom-domain/src/model.rs:446`). (d) D3's precondition paragraph and ADR-141 D6 cite `scripts/reload-published-generation.py`, which is deleted (`crates/loom-facade/src/reload.rs:7-8`). (e) `scripts/dream-build.sh:10` still mentions `npm ci` in a comment after the npm step was removed. All low severity, so close each with a wording fix.

### Dream-machine

1. **DM-01: ADR-0005 and ADR-0007 cite agentbox admission code that no longer exists at the pinned agentbox revision.**
   - **Trigger:** ADR-0005 §2.2 and §6 name `services/dream-engine/src/readiness.rs` (`assess`, `script_paths`, `DarwinSandboxMissing`) and `config.rs` as the annexe admission that refuses darwin without `--sandbox mock|agent`. ADR-0007 §4 relies on the same admission.
   - **Evidence:** at agentbox c57d9c0508f4, `services/dream-engine/src/` has no `readiness.rs`, and no darwin symbol appears in `config.rs`. The service now depends on the external crates `dream-core = "=0.4.0"` and `dream-gate = "=0.3.0"` (`services/dream-engine/Cargo.toml:26-27`). Only `runner.rs:18-26` (`bash -o pipefail`) still matches the ADR.
   - **Consequence:** the reason ADR-0005 gives for keeping the darwin command in config, and for refusing the `=` spellings, cannot be confirmed from either pinned repository. The script's own refusal does still stand: `scripts/darwin-entrypoint.sh:125-129`.
   - **To close:** re-cite the admission at its new home (the dream-core or dream-gate source at the pinned crate version), or amend ADR-0005 to say the admission is external.
2. **DM-02: the darwin entrypoint enforces a decision, "ADR-0008", that has no record.**
   - **Trigger:** `scripts/darwin-entrypoint.sh:36`, `:113-122` refuse (exit 64) any command whose head is not exactly `npx <pin>` and cite "ADR-0008 (structure, not tokens)".
   - **Evidence:** `docs/adrs/` at b77093e564ef stops at ADR-0007, and `docs/adrs/INDEX.md:8-16` lists 0001 to 0007. The 2026-10-08 supply-chain refusal of pre-pin dash arguments (`:88-100`) is also not in any ADR.
   - **Consequence:** the most security-relevant rule on the darwin path (pin-owned resolution) is undocumented, and ADR-0005 §2.2's refusal list understates what the script refuses.
   - **To close:** add ADR-0008 to the series and to INDEX, or re-point the comments at the record that holds it.
3. **DM-03: the closeout extensions on ADR-0001, ADR-0002 and ADR-DL-001 describe a service that has since changed.**
   - **Trigger:** each extension says the Rust service lacks candidate re-evaluation and typed required-check classification, and passes zero-exit FAIL text to the model.
   - **Evidence at agentbox c57d9c0508f4:**
     - `services/dream-engine/src/candidate_flow.rs:1-2` re-runs the required evaluators on the candidate.
     - `engine.rs:1099-1104` vetoes missing, silent, blocked or timed-out baseline evaluators through `gate::environment_vetoes` before any model call.
     - `dispatch.rs:85-89` delegates typed classification to `dream_gate::receipts::classify`.
     - Each extension also pins "current toolkit revision" `7c30573a…`, not b77093e564ef.
   - **Consequence:** the acceptance conditions read as wholly open when the adapter source shows they are at least partly addressed. Whether they are fully met depends on dream-core and dream-gate, which are outside the pinned set.
   - **To close:** re-assess the conditions against the dream-core and dream-gate sources, then update or retire the extensions.
4. **DM-04: ADR-0004 misdescribes the dry streak the agentbox engine now computes.**
   - **Trigger:** ADR-0004 §1 says the engine "counts `INCONCLUSIVE` rows toward [the dry streak], ignoring any other token". The same text appears in `packages/ledger/src/rowContract.ts:32-35`, `packages/ledger/src/index.ts:33-36` and `packages/cli/README.md:47-48`.
   - **Evidence:** at agentbox c57d9c0508f4, `engine.rs:147-150` and `:469-472` say REJECT and INCONCLUSIVE advance the streak, ACCEPT resets it, and BLOCKED-ENV and HANDOFF do neither. The note "the old engine counted only INCONCLUSIVE" marks this as changed by design. `OPERATOR` is not named, and its handling lives in `dream_core::streak` (external).
   - **Consequence:** the decision itself still holds (non-night tokens do not feed the streak), but its stated rationale is stale, and OPERATOR handling cannot be confirmed from pinned source.
   - **To close:** amend the context note, and confirm OPERATOR handling in the dream-core streak source.
5. **DM-05: the ledger write path does not enforce the vocabulary that ADR-0004 defines.**
   - **Trigger:** `dream-machine ledger append --verdict <any>` passes the flag straight through (`packages/cli/src/index.ts:263-279`), and `appendRow` does not validate it (`packages/ledger/src/index.ts:164-167`).
   - **Consequence:** ADR-0004 §3 says non-night tokens are "accepted on read before they are produced on write". In fact the CLI writes any token, including illegal ones, which only `ledger verify` or the row contract catches later.
   - **To close:** validate `verdict` and `evaluated` against `LEDGER_VERDICTS` and `LEDGER_EVALUATED` in `append`.
6. **DM-06: ADR-0005 Test Contract item 2 no longer holds as written.**
   - **Trigger:** item 2 says "the pinned 2026-09-07 leaderboard … reports `ok: true`".
   - **Evidence:** after ADR-0006, that leaderboard fails score uniformity (`packages/cli/src/darwinBounds.ts:223-229`). The in-bound five-candidate case is now asserted on `LB_VARIED` (`packages/cli/src/darwinBounds.test.ts:73-82`), and ADR-0006 §3 says so.
   - **Consequence:** ADR-0005's test contract is stale, although the amendment chain explains why.
   - **To close:** add a note to ADR-0005 or ADR-0006 that the fixture moved.
7. **DM-07: the INDEX's amendment rule is not followed.**
   - **Trigger:** `docs/adrs/INDEX.md:21-22` says an Accepted ADR is "amended by a follow-on ADR … never edited in place".
   - **Evidence:**
     - ADR-0001's Updated line revises its own Test Contract §1 (`ADR-0001:5`, `:182-192`).
     - The Status lines of ADR-0003 and ADR-0006 were edited after acceptance: ADR-0006 was changed in commit 6426583 (#26), after 36ea9c5 (#21) created it.
     - Local closeout sections are appended to Accepted ADRs.
     - ADR-0002 is still `Proposed` even though it shipped and later ADRs depend on it.
   - **Consequence:** status and amendment metadata cannot be relied on mechanically.
   - **To close:** either relax the INDEX rule to allow status-line and addendum edits, or move those edits into follow-on records. Also ratify or re-status ADR-0002.
8. **DM-08: ADR-DL-001 is partly implemented, in shared paths its own Consequences told it to avoid.**
   - **Implements.** proposal (1) as `proposeHarnessChange` and `HARNESS_COMPONENTS` (`packages/ledger/src/index.ts:312-399`), with `ledger signals --propose` (`packages/cli/src/index.ts:96-101`, `:248-254`).
   - **Not implemented:** proposal (2) partly — the five components are named in code but not in `dream.config.json` (`:4-40` are surface-named slots), and the config schema has no component field (`packages/compile/src/config.ts:33-62`). Proposals (3) (held-out dev guard) and (4) (rollback proposals) have no code: there are no hits for held-out, J_dev or rollback in `packages/`.
   - **Consequence:** the ADR says "Fork-local first … fork-namespaced paths", yet the proposer sits in shared `packages/ledger` and `packages/cli`. FORK.md also says everything except the marker is "byte-identical to upstream" (`FORK.md`, table under "How to tell the two apart"), which ADR-0003 to ADR-0007's fork changes in `packages/` contradict as well.
   - **To close:** update DL-001's status to say partly implemented, and correct FORK.md's byte-identity claim.
9. **DM-09: ADR-0001 §2.3 delegation has narrowed.**
   - **Evidence:**
     - The config example and table expect `flywheel` and `redblue` entrypoints, but this repo's `dream.config.json:53-56` declares only `bench` and `darwin`.
     - No `@metaharness/*` package is a dependency or a peer of any package: there are no hits in `package.json` or `packages/*/package.json`, so the "optional/peer" claim in the header is really "invoked via npx only".
     - The compiled prompt's STEP 10–14 (`packages/compile/src/index.ts:260-281`) never names redblue or flywheel.
   - **Consequence:** this is not wrong, since the schema still allows all four (`config.ts:20-29`), but the delegation table describes the possible wiring, not this repo's actual wiring.
   - **To close:** add an addendum stating that the actual wiring is bench and darwin only.
10. **DM-10: the config's darwin mutator URL depends on deployment.**
    - **Evidence:** `dream.config.json:55` points `--ruvllm-url` at `http://127.0.0.1:8084` with model `qwen3.8-27B`. ADR-0007 assumes this is "the Loom door".
    - **Consequence:** that is true only where the annexe runs on the Loom host, which cannot be confirmed from source. The model alias is a legacy name; the estate notes say old qwen3.8 names are still accepted.
    - **To close:** a runtime receipt from the annexe host, which is outside this evidence class.
