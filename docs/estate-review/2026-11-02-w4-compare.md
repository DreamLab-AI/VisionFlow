# Comparison and recommendation index

**Outcome.** This index covers the 163 top-level dated reports, the seven dated `closeout/` reports (none has a comparison section), and VisionFlow ADR-2001 to ADR-2021. It yields 100 rows: 82 comparisons of alternatives and 18 recommendations. Each row is tied to a product path. Recommendations restated across reports are merged into one row, with the originating report cited first. Most recommendations are proposed evaluations rather than measured outcomes. Almost no external comparison in the corpus was executed; the one exception is the oo-cert binary, which was exercised but not benchmarked (COMPARE-F3). At the pins, three product recommendations are already adopted in source: the `/pay` route uses the shared bound NIP-98 verifier, agentbox's decision verifiers fail closed, and agentbox pods ship the forge. Three VisionFlow tooling choices are also confirmed wired in source. Twelve rows are still visibly open in code. They include the forum member-delete NIP-05 leak, the forum edge ACL fall-through, vowl-wasm dropping exact cardinality and the dream automerge without a head-commit match. In two of them a product ADR's rejected alternative is the one actually built: Loom's Postgres client and agentbox's openjev judge. Two accepted VisionFlow ADRs name different sole producers of the sovereign corpus, and both producers run (COMPARE-F1). prose-sanitiser and diagram-ir have no primary-source comparison, and dream-machine has only its own ADR alternatives.

Evidence class: **source tracing at the pinned revision**. No runtime, test or deployment evidence was produced in this pass.

## What users get today, by area

These judgements are taken from the source lines cited in the index, and only from those lines.

- **Governed knowledge.** There is no single end-to-end journey from corpus to answer to correction to receipt. Two compilers publish the corpus (COMPARE-F1). Loom ships no sample corpus, so first use needs a complete bundle (loom:README.md:31). The recommendations would change three measurable things: time to first useful answer, one generation identity per consumer, and journey completion with negative cases refused.
- **VisionClaw payments.** Value-moving `/pay` POSTs are now body-bound and single-use (pay_handler.rs:411-418, nip98.rs:573). Funding receipts and a recoverable paid-job path are still open, and would change lost-response recovery and duplicate charges.
- **Forum.** Deleting a member leaves their NIP-05 name resolving (admins.rs:334-343 against username.rs:382-385). The edge ACL resolver treats an unparseable sidecar as a miss (acl.rs:297-306). MLS is still deferred in the forum's ADR-2016:111-114. The recommendations would change incomplete-removal age and revocation agreement between the native and edge tiers.
- **Ontology viewers.** vowl-wasm loses exact cardinality between parse and graph (mod.rs:170 against builder.rs:60). ontomark-obsidian can save a patch and then reject the call when indexing fails (api.ts:242-253). The recommendations would change construct preservation and the rate of applied-but-unreported writes.
- **dream-engine.** The automerge seam does not pin the evaluated head (seams.rs:481). The recommendation would change verified-head delivery.
- **Website.** SEO watch reads only the first matching robots group (checks.mjs:221-222). The recommendation would change false-negative crawl findings.
- **solid-pod-rs and agentbox pods.** The forge is built into shipped agentbox pods (flake.nix:1548), and the server crate's default features stay empty (Cargo.toml:127), matching ADR-2019.
- **agentbox.** Signed governance decisions and relay events are verified fail-closed (relay-consumer.js:747-754; authority.js:237-240). The judge engine can be laya or openjev, and openjev is pinned to a full commit (agentbox.toml:1025,1031), although the ADR still records it as rejected. Sessions, custody and memory were not judged from source in this pass.
- **Loom.** Loom has no tool-free first use: there is no packaged sample corpus (README.md:31), and the Postgres client is tokio-postgres, not the sqlx its ADR names (Cargo.toml:54).
- **visiontouch-rs.** Nothing reaches users yet. The VisionClaw pin has only a generic haptics toggle (immersion.ts:16), as ADR-2017's implementation status of none says.
- **codescape.** Not judged from source in this pass. The only comparison (Sourcegraph) proposes an evaluation.
- **sidestr-rs.** The README disclaims Lightning-network interoperability (README.md:63), consistent with ADR-2012. Finding statuses are owned by the w4-sidestr re-check.
- **VisionFlow canon.** Pages deploys use pinned actions (deploy.yml:184,197), Pele 1.12.0 is integrity-pinned (VERSION:1-3), and the ADR template carries three status axes. Two of its accepted ADRs contradict each other on the corpus producer (COMPARE-F1).
