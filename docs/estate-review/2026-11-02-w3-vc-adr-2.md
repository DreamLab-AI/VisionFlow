# VisionClaw ADR-2014 to ADR-2027

I traced all 195 headed sections of VisionClaw ADR-2014 to ADR-2027 against the source at VisionClaw `86cdd7635582`. Where a record makes a claim about agentbox, I also read agentbox `c57d9c0508f4`. Thirteen records are operative decisions. ADR-2014 is superseded/historical (ADR-2040 supersedes it, and ADR-2112 supersedes ADR-2040), although its frontmatter still says `accepted`/`live`.

Counted by each row's leading disposition: of the 14 title rows, 11 hold and 3 are partly true or contradicted. Of the 181 sections below the titles:
- 61 are implemented, or their current-fact claims hold. Eight of these also carry a marked Drift, Debt or Tension.
- 23 are partly implemented, contradicted or stale.
- 9 have been overtaken by a later section of the same record.
- 14 are rationale only.
- 74 are dated receipts about earlier commits, which cannot be checked at the pin. Where such a receipt also states a current fact, its row says whether that fact still holds.

The most important finding is that ADR-2027's title, "the shipped compose boots demo-open", now holds only for the dev service. The `visionclaw-production` service is a `cargo build --release` image without dev-auth. It carries none of the demo-open inversions, and it forwards an empty `VISIONCLAW_SECURITY_PROFILE`. No tracked file sets that variable, and in a release build an undeclared profile is fatal before bind. A production compose boot therefore fails closed until the operator declares a profile in their untracked `.env`. `docs/SECURITY-profiles.md:296-302` documents that as the intended migration. The behaviour is safe and deliberate, but the record's title, Decision and Consequences still describe the old behaviour.

Three other findings stand out:
- ADR-2016's "single `reify_activity` primitive" is no longer true. A second PROV-O writer commits to the provenance graph, though it is still insert-only.
- ADR-2021's ban on ad-hoc URN minting is broken by an unparseable `execution` URN in the adapters crate.
- ADR-2022's payload-DID check, `verify_did_matches_challenge`, has no caller.
